Frequently Asked Questions

What charges are included in the calculation?

TradeCalc includes: Brokerage (flat ₹20 or % based on broker), STT (Securities Transaction Tax), Exchange Transaction Charges, SEBI Charges, Stamp Duty, GST on brokerage, and optional capital gains tax (STCG/LTCG). All are shown as separate line items.

What is the difference between STCG and LTCG?

STCG (Short Term Capital Gains) applies to equity held less than 12 months, taxed at 20%. LTCG applies to equity held over 12 months, taxed at 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh per year. Intraday profits are business income, taxed at your income slab rate. F&O profits are also treated as business income.

Which broker does TradeCalc use for calculations?

By default, TradeCalc uses flat ₹20 brokerage per order (matching Zerodha/Groww/Upstox pricing). You can enter a different brokerage amount for other brokers or percentage-based pricing.
