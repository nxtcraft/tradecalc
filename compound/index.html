The power of compounding

  • ₹1L at 12% for 10 years → ₹3.1L (more frequent compounding → more growth)
  • Rule of 72: divide 72 by rate to find years to double (72 ÷ 12 = 6 years)
  • Monthly compounding vs yearly: ~0.5% extra on a 12% rate
  • Start early — 10 years more can double your final corpus
