Rules of thumb

  • Minimum R:R of 1:2 is the standard — risk ₹1 to make ₹2
  • At 1:2 R:R, you only need to win 34% of trades to break even
  • At 1:3, you break even winning just 25% of trades
  • Always check R:R BEFORE entering a trade, not after
